# Get help

label: help

## At the festival

Find the help desk at the Wild & Wired West Virginia venue. Bring the laptop; people there have seen most problems before.

## Online

- The festival website: https://wildandwiredwv.com/
- KDE's guides for the desktop and its apps: https://userbase.kde.org/
- Wild & Wired OS is built from Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, so most Ubuntu answers apply too: https://help.ubuntu.com/

When you ask for help, say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A photo of the screen helps.
