# Start here

label: start here

You are running Wild & Wired OS 26.11, the Linux system made for Wild & Wired West Virginia on November 14, 2026. It is yours to keep, copy, and share.

## Trying it changes nothing

Right now the system runs from the USB stick or disc. Nothing is written to your computer's disk. When you restart, the computer goes back to exactly how it was.

That also means anything you save in this session disappears when you restart. To keep a file, save it to a USB stick or another drive.

## 3 things to try first

1. Open the menu in the bottom left corner, or press the Windows key on your keyboard. Type the name of what you want, such as `firefox` or `files`.
2. Connect to Wi-Fi from the network icon near the clock.
3. Open **Discover** to see the apps you can add with 1 click.

## When you are ready to keep it

Double-click **Install Wild & Wired OS** on the desktop. The section called *Install it* walks through every step.
